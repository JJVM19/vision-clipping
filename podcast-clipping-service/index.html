Frequently asked questions

What is a podcast clipping service?

A service that turns your podcast episodes into short-form clips and distributes them across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X. A done-for-you service like Vision Clipping also runs the accounts, posts on a cadence, and reports on what performed, you just send the episode.

How many clips can you get from one podcast episode?

A typical hour-long episode yields 10 to 30 usable clips, depending on how many distinct, self-contained moments it contains. The limit is the time to cut, caption, format and post them consistently, which is exactly what the service handles.

Do I keep the accounts and audience?

Yes. Vision Clipping distributes on accounts you own and keep, so the audience you build is yours, not borrowed reach on someone else’s profiles.
