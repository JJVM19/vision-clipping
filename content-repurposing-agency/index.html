What a content repurposing agency actually does

Repurposing is the highest-leverage move in content: instead of creating something new, you take what you’ve already said and put it in front of thousands of new people. A content repurposing agency runs that whole process for you, finding the moments in your long-form, cutting them into clips, formatting them per platform, and distributing them consistently.

The gap most founders hit: the editing is the easy part. Running accounts, posting on a cadence across platforms and markets, and knowing what actually worked is a full operations job. That’s the part an agency exists to carry. (For the full workflow, see how to repurpose long-form content.)

Frequently asked questions

What does a content repurposing agency do?

It turns your existing long-form content (podcasts, videos, webinars) into short-form clips and distributes them across social platforms for you, handling the editing, the accounts, the posting cadence, and the reporting, so you get reach without running the operation yourself.

How much does content repurposing cost?

It varies with volume, platforms and markets. Freelance editors charge per clip; managed agencies typically run monthly retainers from around $2,000 to $15,000+, and premium done-for-you partnerships that run a dedicated team on owned accounts start around $10,000 per month.

What content can you repurpose?

Anything with distinct, self-contained moments: podcasts, YouTube videos, webinars, keynotes, livestreams and interviews. A good hour of long-form usually yields 10 to 30 usable short clips.
