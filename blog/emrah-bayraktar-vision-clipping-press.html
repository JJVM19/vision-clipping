Our Co-Founder in the Press

August 2026 · Vision Clipping
In short
  • Vision Clipping co-founder Emrah Bayraktar is one of the operators the major coverage of the clipping economy profiles.
  • He has been featured in NPR, Forbes and Yahoo Finance, and as a guest on the Russell Brunson Show.

When NPR, Forbes and Yahoo Finance set out to explain the clipping economy, they profiled the people who built it. One of them co-founded Vision Clipping. Here is the honest picture of the coverage, with links so you can read it yourself.

The coverage

Our co-founder Emrah Bayraktar has become one of the recognised operators in short-form clipping, and the press reflects it:

  • NPR profiled him in its report on the clipping economy, describing how he went from earning $12 on one clip to running a network of 40,000 clippers.
  • Forbes wrote that he has "personally generated over 400 million views for brands including Andrew Tate, Iman Gadzhi, Luke Belmar," the same calibre of client behind our case studies.
  • Yahoo Finance featured his story in its coverage of clipping as a serious income channel.
  • The Russell Brunson Show hosted him as a guest to break down how clipping actually works.

Being straight about it

We think proof should be honest, so here is the nuance. This coverage is about Emrah and about the clipping industry. It does not name Vision Clipping, because Vision Clipping is the managed agency he went on to co-found to bring that same distribution to founders and brands as a done-for-you service. The expertise the press documented is the expertise now running behind Vision Clipping.
