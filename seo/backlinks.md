# Vision Clipping — Backlink & Citation Plan

The checklist item: *"Heavy SEO article creation, citing us in other articles."* Backlinks are still
one of Google's strongest ranking signals — but the wrong ones (link farms, paid link networks,
mass directory spam) get a site penalised. Everything here is **earned or editorially-placed links**
that survive an algorithm update. Quality and relevance beat volume every time.

**North star:** a few links from real, relevant, trusted sites > hundreds of junk links.

---

## Tier 1 — assets that earn links on their own (do these first)
The cheapest backlinks are the ones people give you because your page is the best answer.

- **The pricing article** (`/blog/how-much-does-a-clipping-agency-cost.html`) is link-bait: writers
  covering creator marketing cite a clear pricing breakdown. Keep it accurate and current.
- **A original-data / stats piece.** We sit on real numbers (3B+ views delivered, per-market reach,
  CPC vs paid ads). A "State of short-form distribution 2026" post with our own data is the single
  most link-worthy asset we can publish — journalists and bloggers link to primary data.
- **Case studies** already exist (`/cases/*`) — pitch each subject's audience/community to reference them.

## Tier 2 — editorial placements (guest posts & contributor articles)
Write genuinely useful articles for sites our buyers already read, with a natural link back.

| Target type | Examples | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / startup media | Indie Hackers, Starter Story, SaaS blogs | "How founders build distribution without a team" |
| Creator-economy publications | ConvertKit/creator blogs, newsletters | Short-form distribution playbooks |
| Marketing / growth blogs | Growth communities, agency roundups | Repurposing long-form, omnipresence |
| Podcasts (show notes = links) | Founder & marketing podcasts | Jaden/Emrah as guests → link in notes |

## Tier 3 — citations, mentions & relationships
- **Digital PR / HARO-style** — respond to journalist requests on content marketing, creator economy,
  short-form video. A quote in a real article = a strong editorial link.
- **Podcast guesting** — every appearance yields a show-notes link + branded search. High ROI given
  the founders' story (3B+ views, named case studies).
- **Partnerships** — tools/agencies adjacent to us (editing tools, CRMs, course platforms) for
  "recommended partners" / integration pages.
- **Unlinked-mention reclamation** — find places already naming "Vision Clipping" and ask for a link.
- **Founder social presence** — link the site from the same real accounts we already run; not a
  ranking link per se, but drives the real branded search + referral traffic that *does* help.

## What to avoid (these hurt)
- Paid link networks / "1,000 backlinks for $50" — footprints Google penalises.
- Mass low-quality directories, blog-comment/forum spam, PBNs.
- Exact-match anchor text at scale (looks manipulated) — vary anchors naturally.
- **Automated/emulator traffic to fake "popularity"** — see the Workstream 3 warning in
  [SEO-PROGRAM.md](SEO-PROGRAM.md). Doesn't build authority; risks the domain.

## Cadence
1. Publish one Tier-1 link-worthy asset per month (data piece or deep guide).
2. 2–3 Tier-2 guest placements / podcast appearances per month once the founders have bandwidth.
3. Track referring domains in Google Search Console (Links report) — measure domains, not raw links.
