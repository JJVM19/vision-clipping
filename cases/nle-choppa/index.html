Rapper with 14.4M monthly listeners, PrankU campaign

01Background

PrankU is a celebrity prank channel from the co-creator of Punk'd, running campaigns with A-list music artists and creators.

NLE Choppa starred in one of the episodes. With 14.4 million monthly listeners, the trailer was going in front of a huge audience.

02Challenge

Every PrankU episode starts as long form. The trailer is the flagship promotion for the episode and goes out on the talent's own Instagram, so it has to hold attention from the very first frame.

03What we did
  1. 01

    Long form in

    We started from the full long form episode.

  2. 02

    High-retention trailer out

    We cut it down into a high-retention trailer, built to hold attention from the first second to the last.

  3. 03

    Posted by the talent

    The trailer went out on his own Instagram as the flagship promotion for the episode.

04Results

The flagship trailer for NLE Choppa's PrankU episode, cut from the long form and posted to his Instagram. Across the PrankU campaigns, our trailers and short form content generated 10.3 million views, and this trailer alone has 859,000 views and 26.9K likes on Instagram.
